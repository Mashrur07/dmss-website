About DMSS

DMSS (Dhumerkuthi Mohammad Ali Shamaj Kalyan Shangastha) is a non-profit social welfare organization dedicated to supporting underprivileged communities. Our goal is to create sustainable change through inclusive social programs and community participation.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower communities by providing education, healthcare support, and social services.

Our Vision

A just and compassionate society where everyone has equal opportunities to thrive.

Our Programs

Education Support

Scholarships, learning materials, and awareness programs for children and youth.

Healthcare Assistance

Medical camps, health awareness sessions, and emergency support.

Community Development

Skill development, social awareness, and community empowerment initiatives.
